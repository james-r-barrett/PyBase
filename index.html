Pyrenoid Evolution Database

Cataloguing pyrenoids across the algae

Bringing together fifty years of scattered ultrastructure literature — presence, absence, and structural detail, tied to phylogeny — to ask when and how often pyrenoids evolved.

Submit a record
Log a species-level observation from a paper you've read — presence/absence, ultrastructure, and the source.
Browse the data
Search reviewed, curated records by species, division, or reference.
