Your record has been logged for review. It isn't visible here or to other submitters — a curator will check it before it's added to the dataset.

This project exists to bring together the pyrenoid literature of the last fifty years — presence, absence, and ultrastructure scattered across decades of electron microscopy papers — into one place, tied to phylogeny, so we can start asking when and how often pyrenoids evolved across the algae.

Records are reviewed before joining the curated dataset. Access to the full backend can be requested by contacting james.barrett@york.ac.uk.
